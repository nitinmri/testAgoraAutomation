Question:  How do I add a tenant COI?
Expected response: To add a tenant COI, open the Tenant COI List and click New COI. Select the tenant, choose the lease, enter a title, upload the document, set expiry notifications, and save the COI. You can also add or edit policies and compliance rules from the COI details screen.
Received response: How to add a tenant COI

To add a tenant COI in MRI Angus, go to Tenant Certificates under Certificates of Insurance, then click New COI.

Select the tenant. You can type at least 3 characters to search and choose from the matches.
Choose the lease from the drop-down list.
Enter a COI title.
Upload the COI document using the browse button. The attachment size limit is 4 MB.
If needed, clear Attach Document to COI Expiry Notification so the document is not included with expiry notices.
In Expiry Notifications, choose whether to notify Employees and/or the Tenant, and set the reminder dates if required. The default reminder dates are 30 and 15 days before expiry.
Choose a signature option:
Employee Signature and select the employee from the drop-down, or
Custom Signature and enter the contact details manually.
Click Create COI.

After you create it, the COI Details screen opens, where you can update general information, contacts, policies, compliance rules, and document attachments.

Note: If the tenant does not appear in the list, their tenant details must have Is COI Required selected first.

If you want, I can also walk you through adding policies or updating compliance rules on the COI Details screen.
